## samling
### presence.circulation.absence
We may state, that in the (far-far) past, as long as physical objects are concerned, the *knowledge* of such one depended on it's circulation. That is, as long as an item didn't circulate as such, it couldn't be known to other than it's posessor. A *Streitaxt* e.g., one of the famous examples of ancient circulations, was passed (like any valuable item today) from hand to hand, generation to generation, circle to circle... and created a virtual space around it that could be described as an early *monetary value*. The axe received an attribute extending it's functionality beyond {can be used to split wood}. The axe holder in return gained an attribute which we would call *status*, depending on the very concrete *biography* of the axe. So here we have our object and it's *object biography* and how the object changes the status of it's posessor by being passed from one to another. With that, the knowledge of the axe and it's value was passed through history until other items of maybe more convenient or just more abstract features (like coins) which also could represent *value* and change a status became used.   
Now what is the feature of such an axe that it was able to alter the state of people or the conditions in which people connected and related to each other? How would we describe that in todays words what the axe actually does to a person? And how is such feature visible on todays physical objects? And where comes (data) into play?
